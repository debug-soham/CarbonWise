Updated just now · How we calculate these
  • Only active accounts count: at least 14 days old, with entries on 5 or more different days. Accounts trusted by an admin always count, and the demo account is never counted.
  • Accounts that look suspicious (a sudden spike, a burst of backdated entries, or repeated values at the input limits) are held back until an admin reviews them.
  • Each account contributes at most 2,000 kg CO₂e per month to the total, so no single account can inflate it.
  • Footprint change compares each user's latest complete month with the average of their first two complete months. We show the median across users, so one unusual account barely moves it.
  • A day is within budget when its total stays under the user's monthly limit divided by the days in that month.
